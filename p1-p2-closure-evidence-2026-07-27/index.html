    Adoption decision

    The original source-level P1/P2 findings are resolved and the AWS pilot now has retained evidence for the durable reference paths above. The overall high-impact production gate remains conditional until the actual deployment retains: real provider/tool IAM simulations, hostile-code escape or microVM evidence, and alert ownership and recovery procedures. These cannot be truthfully inferred from a generic SDK or synthetic pilot role.
